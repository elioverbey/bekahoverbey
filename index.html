About Bekah

Christian. Wife. Mother.
Incorrigible homebody.

Bekah has been writing since she was a little girl, but it wasn't until 2020 — when the world turned upside down and inside out — that she finally thought, why not try to write a book? She quickly learned that writing love stories is as addictive as reading them, and she's never once looked back.

When she's not writing, you can find her wrangling her three wildlings, baking her feelings, and making googly eyes at every baby she comes across.
